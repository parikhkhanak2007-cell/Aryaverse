# ARYAVERSE

### Where India Comes Alive

Aryaverse is a cinematic cultural discovery platform that lets users explore India from state to region to folk story.

Instead of presenting cultural heritage as static information, Aryaverse creates an interactive experience around regional traditions, stories, languages and community participation.

## The Journey

India
→ State
→ Region
→ Folk Story

## Features

- Interactive India map
- State-based cultural exploration
- Regional discovery
- Food, festivals and textiles
- Folk story archive
- Multilingual translation
- Multilingual text-to-speech
- Story-aware AI assistant
- Likes and comments
- Dynamic database-driven content

## Problem

Regional Indian cultural knowledge is often fragmented across languages, locations and disconnected resources.

Aryaverse creates a single immersive discovery experience where users can move from a geographic region into its stories and cultural traditions.

## Why It Matters

Folk traditions are not only historical information. They are living memories carried through language, storytelling, craft, music and community.

Aryaverse aims to make those experiences easier to discover and understand.

## AI Experience

Users can:

1. Translate a folk story.
2. Listen to it in an Indian language.
3. Ask questions about the story.

The AI assistant is instructed to answer from the supplied story rather than inventing story-specific information.
